FAQ

Quick answers

What is the story behind Jolly Match 3? Players restore a candy-themed world affected by a storm.
Can I play without joining a team? Yes, teams are optional but offer extra rewards.
How many helper characters are there? Currently four: Comic, Clumsy, Prankster, and Loafer.
Are there regular new events? Yes, seasonal events are added throughout the year.
Is the game free to play? Yes, with optional in-game purchases available.

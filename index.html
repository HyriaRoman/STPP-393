About the Game

Solve puzzles. Restore the world.

A puzzle world worth rebuilding

Jolly Match 3 tells the story of a candy-filled world scattered by a storm, restored level by level through classic match-3 gameplay. Players solve puzzles to rebuild locations, collect helper characters with unique boosters, and join teams for shared events and rewards.

FAQ

Quick answers

  • What is the story behind Jolly Match 3?

    Players restore a candy-themed world affected by a storm.

    Yes, teams are optional but offer extra rewards.

  • How many helper characters are there?
